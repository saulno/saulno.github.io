---
title: A plain web
date: 2025-10-04
description: In defense of black text on a white page.
---

The oldest web pages still render perfectly. No framework to update, no bundle to rebuild — just hypermedia, the same today as in 1994.

A page is a document, not an app. When I visit yours I want to read it, not negotiate with a cookie banner while three megabytes of JavaScript arrange itself.

Respect for the reader's attention, their eyes.
